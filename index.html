What this means

  • “Few” is reasonable, but it can't be fully proven. The maker, Excite Medical (Tampa, FL), bought the DRX rights from Axiom Worldwide in 2018. Excite publishes no list of owners, and its provider locator returns one location per emailed request.
  • “C” is the cervical (neck) attachment, sold as an add-on or a combination system. It is not a newer table model. Pinto has the DRX 9000C cervical attachment (confirmed by Tim, Oct 8, 2026), so the “9000c” wording is accurate.
  • 4 other clinics advertise neck treatment on a DRX without naming the 9000C, so exact rankings like “one of only 3” don't hold up.
  • DRX use in Colorado is growing (two clinics added one in 2026), so the claim should be rechecked from time to time.
  • No Colorado practice claims to be “the only” DRX provider.

Safer wording for posts & ads

  • “Pinto Chiropractic is one of a small number of Colorado chiropractic offices offering DRX9000® spinal decompression, including the DRX9000C cervical system for neck pain.”
  • “We offer DRX9000® True Non-Surgical Spinal Decompression®, an FDA-cleared system available at relatively few Colorado clinics, for both low back and neck (DRX9000C) conditions.”
